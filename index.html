What is ACHE?

For twenty years, I have studied Acheloios—the ancient Greek god of water, the man-faced bull, and a figure connected with the origins of Greek philosophy and coined money.

So I did something slightly ridiculous but also awesome.

I put Achelous on the blockchain.

ACHE is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Base inspired by that scholarship and by the ancient traditions surrounding Achelous.

The manner and timing of Community distributions may vary as the project develops and will be publicly disclosed.

Humanities scholars and classical numismatists interested in receiving Community ACHE may contact njmolinari@gmail.com with a Base-compatible public wallet address.

A scholarly origin

ACHE takes its identity from Achelous, the mighty water god of Greek religion, conventionally represented as a man-faced bull. The project grows out of Dr. Nicholas J. Molinari's published research on Achelous, Thales, Greek religion, early philosophy, and ancient coinage.
