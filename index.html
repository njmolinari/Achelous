What is ACHE?

For twenty years, I have studied Acheloios—the ancient Greek god of water, the man-faced bull, and a figure connected with the origins of Greek philosophy and coined money.

So I did something slightly ridiculous.

I put the bull on the blockchain.

ACHE is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Base inspired by that scholarship and by the ancient traditions surrounding Achelous.

A scholarly origin

ACHE takes its identity from Achelous, the great river god of Greek religion, conventionally represented as a man-faced bull. The project grows out of Nicholas J. Molinari's published research on Achelous, Thales, Greek religion, early philosophy, and ancient coinage.

ACHE is presented as a cultural and intellectual experiment. Nothing on this site is a promise of future value or financial return.
